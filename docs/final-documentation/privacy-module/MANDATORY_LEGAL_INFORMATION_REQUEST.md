# Mandatory Legal Information Request

The Privacy Module requires the following mandatory legal and organizational information before it can be published and version-frozen.

| Field | Why it is required | Current evidence | Exact information needed | Responsible person | Publication impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal controller name | Identifies the legal entity responsible for data processing (GDPR/DPA requirement). | Missing | Official registered company name | Authorized Company Representative | BLOCKED |
| Official controller address | Required for legal notices and data subject rights enforcement. | Missing | Registered corporate address | Authorized Company Representative | BLOCKED |
| Official privacy email | Primary contact for privacy inquiries and DSRs. | Missing | e.g. privacy@rentipid.com | Privacy Contact | BLOCKED |
| Privacy contact or office | The specific person or department handling privacy matters. | Missing | Title or department name | Compliance Officer | BLOCKED |
| DPO designation status | Determines if a formal Data Protection Officer is legally required. | Missing | FORMALLY_DESIGNATED_AND_VERIFIED or NOT_YET_DESIGNATED | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED |
| DPO name | Identifies the DPO if formally designated. | Missing | Full legal name | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED (if designated) |
| DPO email | Contact for the formal DPO. | Missing | e.g. dpo@rentipid.com | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED (if designated) |
| Policy owner | Internal owner responsible for maintaining the privacy policy. | Missing | Title of the policy owner | Compliance Officer | BLOCKED |
| Legal or compliance reviewer | Identifies the person who approved the legal text. | Missing | Name and Title | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED |
| Reviewer approval date | Documents when the review occurred. | Missing | Date (YYYY-MM-DD) | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED |
| Authorized publication approver | Person authorizing the live deployment of the policy. | Missing | Name and Title | Authorized Company Representative | BLOCKED |
| Approved effective date | The date the policy legally takes effect. | Missing | Date (YYYY-MM-DD) | Compliance Officer | BLOCKED |
| Verified hosting provider | Discloses where data is physically hosted. | Missing | Name of cloud provider | Engineering Lead | BLOCKED |
| Verified database provider | Discloses the primary database processor. | Missing | Name of DB provider | Engineering Lead | BLOCKED |
| Verified storage provider | Discloses the blob/file storage processor. | Missing | Name of storage provider | Engineering Lead | BLOCKED |
| Verified payment provider and status | Discloses payment processors (e.g. PayMongo). | Missing | Name and LIVE/SANDBOX status | Finance Admin | BLOCKED |
| Verified KYC provider and status | Discloses identity verification processors. | Missing | Name and LIVE/SANDBOX status | Compliance Officer | BLOCKED |
| Verified AI provider and status | Discloses automated processing and AI sub-processors. | Missing | Name and LIVE/SANDBOX status | Engineering Lead | BLOCKED |
| Approved retention bases | Legal justification for data retention periods. | Missing | Retention schedules | Legal Counsel | BLOCKED |
| Cross-border processing confirmation | Discloses international data transfers. | Missing | Jurisdiction list | Compliance Officer | BLOCKED |

Please provide this information to proceed with publication and version freeze.
