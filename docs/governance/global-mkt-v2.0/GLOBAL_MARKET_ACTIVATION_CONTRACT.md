# RENTipid GLOBAL-MKT / v2.0 — Global Market Activation Contract

## 1. Controlling Invariant & Permanent Policy

This document establishes the permanent, legally and technically binding standard for commercial activation in RENTipid.

**GOLDEN RULE OF GLOBAL MARKETPLACE ACTIVATION:**
> **A RENTipid jurisdiction is NOT commercially ACTIVE merely because country selection exists, language translation exists, display currency quoting exists, or GLCC Production availability exists.**
> 
> **A jurisdiction becomes GLOBAL-MKT COMMERCIAL ACTIVE if and only if a verified local renter and provider in that jurisdiction can complete the full approved 13-capability end-to-end marketplace lifecycle in production.**

---

## 2. The Mandatory 13-Capability Market Lifecycle Standard

Every registered jurisdiction must satisfy and pass verification across all 13 core lifecycle capabilities prior to receiving commercial active status:

1. **Account Registration (C01):** Local residents can register as renters or providers under country-appropriate legal terms.
2. **KYC & Identity Verification (C02):** Providers and renters pass jurisdiction-compliant identity and anti-fraud verification.
3. **Local Listing Publication (C03):** Providers can create and publish compliant rental listings situated within the jurisdiction's physical borders.
4. **Market Pricing Authority (C04):** Listings are legally priced, stored, and audited in the authorized domestic transaction currency.
5. **Search & Discovery (C05):** Local listings appear in radius, geographic, and keyword searches with localized formatting.
6. **Booking & Rental Lifecycle (C06):** Complete reservation, deposit hold, turnover inspection, return inspection, and completion workflow.
7. **In-App & Multi-Channel Communications (C07):** Both parties receive localized in-app messages, SMS alerts, and email notifications.
8. **Approved Payment Gateway (C08):** Renters can pay through domestic payment methods and authorized payment gateways available in that market.
9. **Automated Provider Payout (C09):** Providers reliably receive automated net rental payouts in their designated local bank accounts or digital wallets.
10. **Deposit, Claims, Refunds & Disputes (C10):** Security deposits are safely held, refunded, or arbitrated through formalized damage claims and dispute protocols.
11. **Multilingual Interface (C11):** Users interact in their preferred supported language with zero raw keys, zero fallback leaks, and zero Unicode corruptions.
12. **Localized Display Currency (C12):** Prices and fee breakdowns are displayed with proper mathematical rounding and currency symbols.
13. **Jurisdictional Regulatory Compliance (C13):** Prohibited categories, local licensing rules, tax withholding, and consumer protection mandates are strictly enforced.

---

## 3. Strict Disqualification & Anti-False-Active Rules

1. **No Language/Currency Conflation:** Translating the platform into a country's national language or quoting prices in its currency does NOT activate the market.
2. **No Payout Omission:** A jurisdiction CANNOT be declared active if renters can pay but providers cannot receive automated payouts. Payment without payout is strictly prohibited.
3. **No KYC Bypasses:** If local law mandates identity verification, listings cannot be published or payments disbursed without validated KYC evidence.
4. **No Simulated Acceptance:** Test-mode transactions, fake bank receipts, or localhost demonstrations cannot be substituted for actual deployed production acceptance.
