## 1. Executive Summary & Controlling Governance Standard

- **Total Jurisdictions Inventoried:** **46** (100% of GLCC registered countries)
- **GLCC Production Available:** **46 / 46 (100%)**
- **Commercially Active Jurisdictions:** **0 / 46 (0%)**
- **Governing Standard:** In accordance with the Project Owner's explicit directive, **GLCC Production Available != Commercially Active**. No jurisdiction may be marked commercially active until all 13 core lifecycle capabilities are validated end-to-end.

## 4. Regional Market Summaries

### 4.1 Asia-Pacific (APAC) — 12 Countries
- **Countries:** Philippines (`PH`), Australia (`AU`), Singapore (`SG`), Malaysia (`MY`), Indonesia (`ID`), Vietnam (`VN`), Japan (`JP`), South Korea (`KR`), India (`IN`), China (`CN`), Thailand (`TH`), etc.
- **Current Status:** Foundation established in GLCC; Philippines has partial operational readiness under legacy PayMongo/manual flows. Mainland China carries 2 deferred blockers (`CN-BLK-001`, `CN-BLK-002`). Thailand requires PromptPay and local KYC/payout rails.

### 4.2 Americas (AMERICAS) — 3 Countries
- **Countries:** United States (`US`), Canada (`CA`), Brazil (`BR`).
- **Current Status:** English, French, and Portuguese localization active; requires regional gateway orchestration (Stripe/Adyen/Pix) and address adapters.

### 4.3 Europe, Middle East & Africa (EMEA) — 2 Countries
- **Countries:** United Kingdom (`GB`), United Arab Emirates (`AE`).
- **Current Status:** English and Arabic RTL active; requires UK Faster Payments/Bacs and UAE CBUAE compliant payment/payout orchestration.

### 4.4 European Union / EEA (EU_EEA) — 29 Countries
- **Countries:** Germany (`DE`), France (`FR`), Italy (`IT`), Spain (`ES`), Netherlands (`NL`), Belgium (`BE`), Austria (`AT`), Ireland (`IE`), Portugal (`PT`), Poland (`PL`), Sweden (`SE`), Denmark (`DK`), Finland (`FI`), Greece (`GR`), Czech Republic (`CZ`), Romania (`RO`), Hungary (`HU`), Norway (`NO`), Iceland (`IS`), Luxembourg (`LU`), Bulgaria (`BG`), Croatia (`HR`), Cyprus (`CY`), Estonia (`EE`), Latvia (`LV`), Lithuania (`LT`), Malta (`MT`), Slovakia (`SK`), Slovenia (`SI`), Liechtenstein (`LI`).
- **Current Status:** Common GDPR compliance baseline active; requires SEPA payment/payout integration and DAC7 marketplace reporting framework.
