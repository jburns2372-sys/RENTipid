## 1. Governance & Authorization Policy

This Risk Register establishes the initial comprehensive risk inventory for RENTipid.

> **Project Owner Authorization Rule:** Automated agents cannot accept residual risks on behalf of the platform owner. All risks where residual risk remains Moderate or High and requires organizational tolerance are explicitly marked:  
> `RISK DECISION: PROJECT OWNER AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED`.

Scoring adheres to `ISO_RISK_METHODOLOGY.md` ($\text{Risk} = \text{Likelihood } [1\text{--}5] \times \text{Impact } [1\text{--}5]$):
* **Low (1–4)** | **Moderate (5–9)** | **High (10–16)** | **Critical (17–25)**
