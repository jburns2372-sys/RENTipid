## 1. Scope & Legal Neutrality Policy

This register catalogues all categories of user, business, transaction, and operational data processed within the RENTipid ecosystem.

> **Legal Neutrality Rule:** In accordance with program rules, no legal conclusions may be fabricated by automated agents. Where the lawful basis under regional regulations (e.g. GDPR, Philippines DPA 2012, Thailand PDPA, Singapore PDPA) requires formal legal counsel review, the status is explicitly recorded as `REQUIRES HUMAN/LEGAL EVIDENCE`.

## 3. Evidence and legal limits

21 categories, including conditional records. All 21 lawful bases require human/legal evidence. Controller is RENTipid's proposed organizational role; legal allocation needs counsel. Schema presence proves capability, not populated production records. Pseudonymized data remains PII. No new numeric retention schedule, statutory deadline, automated deletion or lawful basis is approved. EVD-17/19 and docs/final-documentation/privacy-module/MANUAL_RETENTION_AND_DISPOSAL_RUNBOOK.md govern manual v1 retention, approved automation deferral, legal holds, active obligations, DPO review and separate Production disposal authorization.

DAT-07 is conditional, not proof raw KYC documents are stored or never stored by RENTipid. Provider-processed raw identity evidence differs from retained applicant/status/reference metadata. EVD-21 real smoke used synthetic applicants and no real document upload.
