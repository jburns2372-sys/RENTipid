# RENTipid Global Legal Compliance Register & In-App Positioning Blueprint v1.0

This document defines the official compliance rules and jurisdiction configurations for the RENTipid platform.

## 1. RENTipid Legal Stance

"RENTipid is designed and operated to comply with applicable provisions of Philippine laws and applicable jurisdiction-specific international laws where their territorial, material, provider, category, feature, or transaction conditions apply."

## 2. Jurisdiction Status Model

- **SUPPORTED**: Market is formally launched; full regulatory compliance stack is verified and localized.
- **VALIDATION_REQUIRED**: Traffic is permitted, but localized features (e.g., specific tax reporting, advanced data residency) are pending external legal sign-off.
- **COMPLIANCE_READY**: Infrastructure supports the jurisdiction, but launch is pending business decision.
- **ACTIVE**: Default state for the Philippine baseline.
- **RESTRICTED**: Only specific transaction categories (e.g., cross-border B2B) are permitted; consumer transactions blocked.
- **BLOCKED**: IP/Geography is geofenced; registration and transactions are prohibited due to sanctions or untenable regulatory risk.
